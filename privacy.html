Privacy & video choices

Updated November 17, 2026

In the Dome is owned and operated by Jordan Ellams and Michael Wilson. This is a static website: there are no user accounts, checkout forms, newsletter sign-ups, ad pixels, or audience analytics scripts, and no YouTube player loads unless you choose to load it.

What happens when you visit

You can browse inthedome.ca without giving us personal information. Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard technical request details—such as IP address, browser type, and requested page—to deliver the site reliably and keep it secure. We do not build advertising profiles or run tracking scripts on this site.

YouTube players and your choice

Episode preview photos are hosted directly on inthedome.ca—no YouTube iframe or Google script loads until you click “Load YouTube player.” Choosing to load a player connects your browser to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), where Google may collect device, network, and playback data under Google’s Privacy Policy.

You can click “Remove loaded YouTube players” on the homepage at any time to unload active players, or watch via the direct YouTube link instead.

Email inquiries & privacy requests

Clicking a contact or sponsorship link opens your email app to message In the Dome directly. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply and handle your inquiry—never to add you to a marketing list. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details by email.

For sponsorship, website, or privacy requests (including access, correction, or deletion of an inquiry you sent), email Jordan Ellams and Michael Wilson directly at inthedomepod@gmail.com.

Updates

If we ever add accounts, forms, analytics, or payments in a future release, we will update this notice before turning them on.
