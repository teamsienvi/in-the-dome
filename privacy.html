Privacy & video choices

Updated November 12, 2026

You can browse this website without creating an account. We do not load a YouTube player until you choose to load it. This version has no newsletter sign-up, payment checkout, advertising pixels or audience analytics scripts.

Who this notice covers

This notice covers the In the Dome website at inthedome.ca. Jordan Ellams and Michael Wilson own In the Dome; SIENVI Agency supports the website and commercial inquiries. YouTube and other linked services have their own privacy practices.

Information used to operate the website

When the site is hosted on Vercel, serving and securing a request may involve technical information such as an IP address, browser details, the page requested and request time. We use hosting services to deliver the site and address security or technical problems. We do not add an advertising profile or audience-tracking identifier through this website’s code.

When you contact us

Email links open your email application. If you send a message, the recipient receives your email address, message and any details you include. SIENVI Agency may coordinate a response with the In the Dome hosts when relevant to your request. Use only the information needed for your inquiry; please do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal records.

Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing list. Any future marketing subscription would need a separate choice and an unsubscribe option.

YouTube players and your choice

No YouTube player, thumbnail request or Google script is loaded by this site before you choose “Load YouTube player.” That choice connects your browser to Google/YouTube to show the video. Google may receive device, network and viewing information and use cookies or similar technology under its own policies. The player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain; this does not mean playback is anonymous or free of data processing.

Use the direct YouTube link instead if you prefer. On the homepage, “Remove loaded YouTube players” removes the loaded frames and restores the choice buttons. It stops future player loads from those frames, but does not erase information already transmitted or cookies set by YouTube. You can manage those through your browser and Google privacy controls. Your player choice is not saved between page visits by this website.

Service providers and storage

Website hosting, email and optional YouTube playback involve service providers. Information may be processed outside your province or Canada, where other laws may apply. GitHub is used to manage website source code; it is not a visitor submission form. This public version does not send visitor records to a Supabase database or provide sponsor accounts.

We retain inquiry information for responding and related records, and keep it only as long as needed for those purposes or applicable recordkeeping obligations. Technical service information is also subject to the provider’s configured retention. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed secure.

Questions, access, correction and complaints

To ask about information you supplied, request access or correction, ask about deletion, or raise a privacy concern, email info@sienvi.com with the subject “In the Dome privacy request.” SIENVI Agency will help route your request to the responsible show owner. We may need reasonable verification and may be required to retain some records. Applicable rights and legal exceptions govern how a request is handled.

If a concern is unresolved, you can contact the appropriate privacy regulator. Canadian resources include the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, OIPC British Columbia and OIPC Alberta.

Changes and other services

We will update this notice before adding materially different data collection, such as an account, sponsor form, analytics or payments. Review the notice again when using new features.
