Privacy & video choices

Updated November 17, 2026

In the Dome is owned and operated by Jordan Ellams and Michael Wilson. This website has no user accounts, payment checkouts, newsletter lists, or advertising pixels. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site traffic, a simple sponsorship inquiry form to reach the hosts, and click-to-load YouTube players.

Hosting & website analytics (GA4)

Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard request details—such as IP address, browser type, and requested page—to deliver the site reliably and keep it secure.

We also use Google Analytics 4 (G-X21CNC0YM0) to measure aggregate page visits and general site usage so we know which content resonates with listeners. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies and sends page-view data to Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not use advertising remarketing pixels.

·

YouTube players and your choice

Episode preview photos are hosted directly on inthedome.ca—no YouTube iframe loads until you click “Load YouTube player.” Choosing to load a player connects your browser to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), where Google may collect device, network, and playback data.

You can click “Remove loaded YouTube players” on the homepage at any time to unload active players, or watch via the direct YouTube link instead.

Sponsorship form & email inquiries

When you submit our sponsorship inquiry form or email us directly, the details you enter (name, email, company, optional phone, and message) are sent directly to In the Dome at inthedomepod@gmail.com so we can respond to your inquiry. Submitting an inquiry never adds you to a marketing mailing list. Please do not submit passwords or payment-card details.

To request access, correction, or deletion of an inquiry you sent, email Jordan Ellams and Michael Wilson at inthedomepod@gmail.com.

Updates

If we ever add user accounts, newsletter sign-ups, or payments in a future release, we will update this notice before turning them on.
